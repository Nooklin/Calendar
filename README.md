<h2 align="center">🎯 Our Mission</h2>

<p align="center"><b><i>Your calendar should stay yours.</i></b></p>

<p align="center">Nooklin Calendar keeps your events in a database on your phone. Plan your days in month, week, day and overview views, get reminders and widgets, and follow other calendars by link or CalDAV when you choose to. Nothing is sent anywhere except to the addresses you add yourself. Part of the <a href="https://github.com/Nooklin">Nooklin</a> suite of free Android apps.</p>

<h2 align="center">✨ Features</h2>

- Month, week, day and overview views; the month has four layouts (Compact, Stacked, Details, List)
- Events with repeats (including custom repeats), several reminders each, and the time zone they were made in
- Reminders as a notification with Snooze and Dismiss, set again after a restart
- Date and time typed in the title show as chips above the keyboard (English and Dutch), time picked with wheels, and quick buttons for the length of an event
- Travel time for an event (5 minutes to 2 hours): a light block in the week and day view, and a reminder that goes off that much earlier
- Dutch public holidays as a calendar, which you can turn off
- Widgets: today, week, month, date, up next (small and wide), day planner, list, and today and tomorrow, plus a Quick add tile
- Dashboard with twelve cards (tonight, free evenings, up next, free blocks, overlaps, find time and more), which you can reorder and set up
- Search in title, place and notes
- Import and export of .ics files, with repeats, time zones and reminders
- Calendars by link (.ics, https:// or webcal://, read only) and CalDAV accounts (Nextcloud, ownCloud, Radicale, Baikal, Synology, Fastmail, iCloud, Posteo or other), with changes in both directions
- Background sync of followed calendars, with a choice of how often and an option for Wi-Fi only
- App lock with your fingerprint, face or screen lock, screenshots off, and reminders hidden on the lock screen
- Light, dark or black theme, an accent colour, the Inter, Work Sans or system font, and optional vibration feedback
- A tour at the first start and a Help and Support page in the settings

<h2 align="center">🔒 Privacy</h2>

- Your calendars and events are stored in a database on the phone and nowhere else.
- The app asks for the internet only to download calendars you add by a link and to sync a CalDAV account you connect, and only with those addresses.
- A CalDAV password and the address of a link calendar are stored encrypted with the Android Keystore.
- No analytics, crash reporting or telemetry of any kind.
- No Google Play Services dependency.
- The four permissions are: showing notifications, setting an alarm at the exact minute, setting the alarms again after the phone restarts (all for reminders), and the internet for links and CalDAV.

<h2 align="center">🧭 What you need to do yourself</h2>

Some features do nothing until you set them up:

- **Reminders:** allow notifications when Android asks. Without that permission you get no reminders.
- **Widgets:** add them from the widget list of your home screen. The Quick add tile is added in the quick settings panel.
- **Calendars by link or CalDAV:** open Settings, Calendars, and choose Subscribe to an .ics link or Connect a CalDAV account.
- **Background sync:** it is on by default, every hour. Change it under Settings, Calendars, Background sync.
- **App lock:** it uses the screen lock of your phone, so set one up in Android first.
- **Dutch holidays:** they show by default. Turn them off under Settings, Calendar.

<h2 align="center">⚠️ Known limits</h2>

- A calendar by link is read only and cannot use a login.
- A repeating event with changed days on a CalDAV server is read only.
- Travel time is kept in .ics files and on CalDAV servers only between Nooklin apps; other apps ignore it.
- A reminder shows the start time of the event, not the time you have to leave.
- Android decides when background work runs, so a sync can be later than the time you chose; widgets are redrawn about every 15 minutes.
- The Wi-Fi only option needs a connection that Android does not count as metered.
- The interface is in English.
- Release builds are not minified (R8 is off).

<h2 align="center">🛠️ Build</h2>

Requires Android Studio and JDK 25 (see `gradle/gradle-daemon-jvm.properties`), Android 13 (API 33) or newer to run.

```
.\gradlew assembleDebug
```

Tests: `.\gradlew testDebugUnitTest`.

For a signed release, copy `keystore.properties.example` to `keystore.properties`, fill it in, then run `.\gradlew assembleRelease`. Never commit the keystore or `keystore.properties`.

<h2 align="center" id="verify-a-release-apk">✅ Verify a release APK</h2>

```
apksigner verify --print-certs calendar-v1.0.0.apk
```

Check the file hash against `SHA256.txt` on the release page with `Get-FileHash calendar-v1.0.0.apk` (PowerShell) or `sha256sum calendar-v1.0.0.apk`. The signing certificate should be the same in every release.
